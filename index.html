About

Welcome to my webpage! I am a Chemical Engineering Ph.D. candidate at the University of Maryland College Park, under the supervision of Dr. Srinivasa R. Raghavan. Prior to that, I worked as a software developer for 2 years at Citicorp Services in Pune, India. I received my bachelor's degree in Chemical Engineering from Indian Institiute of Technology Kanpur in 2019.

My research focuses on designing innovative formulations, from hydrogels that achieve precise drug release kinetics to plastic materials that can degrade on-demand. I'm passionate about translating fundamental science into real-world applications, and I hold two U.S. patents. I am currently collaborating with several startups, including Medcura, where we aim to develop "smart" hemostatic materials.

I am currently on the job market, seeeking opportunities in drug formulation and development, advanced materials, and polymer science.
